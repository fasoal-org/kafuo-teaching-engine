# Arabic Language Teaching

Purpose: academically accurate Arabic language instruction through structured explanation, authentic linguistic examples, grammatical analysis, comparison and independent application.

## How this Skill works in the lesson

- This is the PRIMARY Skill of every teaching slide at this stage. Its subject method leads how the slide teaches. A pedagogical Skill (UbD, Feynman and the like) may support it, never replace it.
- The Teaching Model Flow instructions and the slide's role (explanation, worked_example, check_understanding, summary, transition) decide which slides exist, what each slide covers, and their order. Never add, merge, split or reorder slides because of this Skill.
- The grounded textbook content is the authority for facts, definitions, notation, examples and answers. That means the cited Content Units and their book kinds: key concept, worked example, check, prior knowledge, exercise. Apply the method below only where that content supports it.
- Follow the lesson's language directive and narration rules for the spoken register.

## Shared principles

Academic accuracy
- Follow the approved curriculum content and this slide's learning objective.
- Keep definitions, terminology, notation, formulas and relationships exactly as the textbook states them.
- Never invent facts, data, examples or results and present them as textbook content. An illustration of your own must be correct and must not contradict the book.

Clear explanation
- Say what an idea means and why it matters before using it.
- Introduce an unfamiliar term or symbol before relying on it.
- Connect the step to the prerequisite it needs.
- No repetition, no filler.

Progressive teaching
- Move from the foundation to the application.
- Justify each important reasoning step instead of stating an answer.
- Use a visual only when it makes a relationship, process, structure or comparison clearer.

Worked examples
- Name the given information and what is required.
- Say why the chosen method fits.
- Work in meaningful steps.
- Check the result when a check is possible.

Common mistakes
- Address a mistake only when it is real and relevant to this content.
- Say why it is wrong and contrast it with the correct reasoning, at the point where it would happen.
- Never invent a misconception to fill a slot.

Independent understanding
- Link the explanation to the lesson's own checks and practice.
- Never reveal answers to the quiz or question-bank items the learner answers alone.
- A solution the Teaching Model Flow asks for on this slide is part of teaching, and you write it in full. An example is the solution slide for a «تحقق من فهمك» item.

## Arabic language method

1. **Language of the explanation.** Write the generated educational explanations in clear, academically appropriate Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى).
2. **Branch first.** Identify the lesson's branch and learning objective from the grounded content before applying a teaching pattern: نحو وصرف (grammar and syntax), قراءة وفهم (reading comprehension), أدب (literature), or كتابة وتعبير (writing and expression). Apply only that branch's pattern below.
3. **Grammar and syntax (النحو والصرف).**
   - Introduce the grammatical rule as the textbook states it.
   - Explain its terminology, for example المبتدأ والخبر، الفاعل، المفعول به، الحال.
   - Present correct example sentences, taken from the textbook where it gives them.
   - Analyse the sentence components and their grammatical functions.
   - Explain the inflection markers (علامات الإعراب): the case or mood, its sign, and why.
   - Show how a grammatical operator (العامل) such as كان وأخواتها، إنّ وأخواتها، حروف الجر والنصب والجزم changes the sentence structure, before and after.
   - Compare similar rules when it helps.
   - Highlight common parsing and inflection mistakes.
4. **Reading comprehension (القراءة والفهم).**
   - Set the context of the text.
   - Explain the important vocabulary in context.
   - Identify the main idea and the supporting information.
   - Show interpretation and inference that rest on the text.
   - Distinguish what the text states explicitly from what is inferred.
5. **Literature (الأدب والنصوص).**
   - Introduce the relevant literary context: the author, the era, the genre.
   - Explain the selected text or literary concept.
   - Analyse its linguistic and rhetorical features (الصور البيانية، المحسنات البديعية، الأسلوب).
   - Tie every interpretation to evidence quoted from the text.
6. **Writing and expression (الكتابة والتعبير).**
   - Explain the writing objective.
   - Present a suitable model.
   - Demonstrate organisation, expression and revision.
   - Highlight relevant language and structural mistakes.
   - Support the learner's own independent writing.
7. **Representations.** Use a comparison table for related grammatical rules. Use visual annotation for sentence analysis: mark each word with its function and inflection sign.
8. **Why it is wrong.** Explain why an incorrect construction is incorrect: which rule it breaks, and what the correct form is. Never just replace it with the correct answer.
9. **Diacritics.** Keep the correct diacritics (التشكيل) wherever they establish grammatical or semantic meaning, especially on inflection endings and on words that are ambiguous without them. Never alter the diacritics of a Qur'anic verse, a hadith or a quoted text, and always quote it exactly as the source gives it.

## By slide role

- **explanation:** the rule, concept or text in its branch's pattern, with a correct example and its analysis.
- **worked_example:** an authentic sentence or passage → the steps of the analysis (إعراب, inference, or rhetorical analysis) → the justified conclusion.
- **check_understanding:** set the items clearly. Give no answers unless the flow instructions ask for them.
- **summary:** the rule or skill in brief, a comparison with the rule it is often confused with, and the most common mistake.

Do not apply the grammar teaching sequence to reading, literature or writing lessons.
