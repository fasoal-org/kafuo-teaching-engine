### Saying notation in this lesson's language

Say notation in the lesson's own spoken language, never in Arabic words. For English speech, for example: `x²` → "x squared", `3/4` → "three quarters", `x ≥ 5` → "x is greater than or equal to five", `F = ma` → "F equals m a", `9.8 m/s²` → "nine point eight metres per second squared", `H₂O` → "H two O".
