**Purpose of this slide.** Illustrate an idea or method through one concrete case.

**It must contain:**
- the case, question or instance, stated clearly
- how to approach or solve it
- the reasoning behind the approach
- the link back to the concept or rule it illustrates

**What leads, what supports.** The case leads; approach, reasoning and the link to the concept support it. Steps may be shown but need not be decomposed to worked-example depth.

**Learner engagement.** Let the learner see the idea working in a real instance.

**Must NOT appear:**
- a hint-only slide
- an answer-only display with no reasoning
- an unrelated second example

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
