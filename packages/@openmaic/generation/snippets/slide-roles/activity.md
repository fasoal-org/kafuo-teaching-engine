**Purpose of this slide.** Have the learner build understanding by doing something themselves.

**It must contain:**
- what the learner is asked to do, stated clearly
- the focus, question or material the activity works on
- the actions or prompts that guide the doing
- what the learner should notice, record or produce, and how to make sense of it

**What leads, what supports.** The task leads; the actions or prompts form the body; the sense-making prompt closes it.

**Learner engagement.** The learner does the thinking and the finding out — the slide sets it up.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the finding, conclusion, analysis or finished work the activity asks for
- materials or steps the activity does not need

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
