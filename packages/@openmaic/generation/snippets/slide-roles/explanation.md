**Purpose of this slide.** Present new knowledge — one idea, term, rule or observation — so the learner genuinely understands it.

**It must contain:**
- the one idea this slide teaches, named clearly
- the substance of its explanation — full sentences where the idea needs them
- the supporting ideas, conditions or evidence that make it make sense
- a key takeaway
- a useful visual or a small supporting example when it adds understanding

**What leads, what supports.** The idea and its core explanation lead; supporting ideas and the takeaway are visibly subordinate.

**Learner engagement.** Depth, not breadth: help the learner understand what it is, why it holds, and when it applies.

**Must NOT appear:**
- a second, unrelated idea
- a list of keywords with no explanation
- a full worked problem or a step-by-step procedure standing in for the explanation

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
