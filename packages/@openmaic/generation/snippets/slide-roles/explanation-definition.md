**Purpose of this slide.** Teach what a term means.

**It must contain:**
- the term
- a focused teaching definition in learner-appropriate language
- its meaning or context — where and why the term is used
- optionally a micro-example, a useful visual, or a key takeaway when it improves understanding

**What leads, what supports.** The term and its definition lead; context and any micro-example support.

**Learner engagement.** Help the learner recognise the term in use, not merely recite it.

**Must NOT appear:**
- an isolated dictionary-style definition with no teaching context
- several unrelated terms

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
