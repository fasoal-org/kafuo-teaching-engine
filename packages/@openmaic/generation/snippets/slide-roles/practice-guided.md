**Purpose of this slide.** Let the learner apply what was already taught, with support visible from the start.

**It must contain:**
- the practice task
- visible scaffolding from the beginning — hints, a partial structure, shared first steps, or guiding prompts
- a clear expected response (what the learner should produce)

**What leads, what supports.** The task leads; the scaffolding is clearly attached to it; the expected response is explicit.

**Learner engagement.** The learner completes the work; the scaffolding lowers the first step, it does not finish the task.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the full solution or final answer
- new content that was not taught earlier in the lesson

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
