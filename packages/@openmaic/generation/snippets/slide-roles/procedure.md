**Purpose of this slide.** Teach a reusable sequence for performing a method or process.

**It must contain:**
- the goal of the procedure
- prerequisites or materials, only when they are required
- ordered, executable steps, each with a short explanation where useful
- genuinely important tips or warnings
- the expected result

**What leads, what supports.** The goal leads; the numbered steps are the body; tips/warnings and the expected result support.

**Learner engagement.** Each step must be something the learner can actually do.

**Must NOT appear:**
- one specific problem solved (that is a worked example)
- vague steps that cannot be executed
- warnings that are not genuinely important

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
