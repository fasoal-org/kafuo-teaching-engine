**Purpose of this slide.** Teach an observation grounded in something the learner can actually examine.

**It must contain:**
- the observable basis — the evidence, data, source or visual being observed
- what to notice (direct the learner's attention)
- the observation itself, stated clearly
- why it matters

**What leads, what supports.** The observable basis and what to notice lead; the stated observation and its significance follow.

**Learner engagement.** Make the learner look first, then name what was seen.

**Must NOT appear:**
- a claim with no observable basis
- decorative imagery unrelated to the observation

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
