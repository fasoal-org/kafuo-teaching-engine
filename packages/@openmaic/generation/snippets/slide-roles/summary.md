**Purpose of this slide.** Consolidate what the lesson taught.

**It must contain:**
- the lesson's most important ideas — normally three to five
- the relationships among them, or a visual synthesis, when valuable
- a clear overall takeaway

**What leads, what supports.** The key ideas lead as a clearly structured set; the takeaway closes the slide.

**Learner engagement.** The learner should recognise everything here from the lesson.

**Must NOT appear:**
- ANY new concept the lesson has not taught
- a re-teaching of details
- a generic 'thank you' page

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
