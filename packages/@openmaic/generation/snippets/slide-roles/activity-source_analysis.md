**Purpose of this slide.** Have the learner analyse a source themselves.

**It must contain:**
- the source itself, or a clear reference to it (text, image, data, map, document)
- contextual framing when the learner needs it to approach the source
- focused prompts that elicit evidence extraction, interpretation, inference or justification

**What leads, what supports.** The source leads; framing and the analysis prompts support it.

**Learner engagement.** The learner performs the analysis; the prompts direct their attention without answering.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the analysis, interpretation or conclusion itself — do NOT give the analysis
- generic prompts that could apply to any source

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
