**Purpose of this slide.** Have the learner build understanding by investigating something themselves.

**It must contain:**
- the investigation question
- what the learner is exploring
- the actions to perform
- what to observe or record
- how to reflect on or interpret the result
- materials or a hypothesis ONLY when the investigation actually requires them

**What leads, what supports.** The question leads; actions and what-to-record form the body; the interpretation prompt closes it.

**Learner engagement.** The learner does the finding out — the slide sets it up.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the finding or conclusion of the investigation
- materials or hypotheses the investigation does not need

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
