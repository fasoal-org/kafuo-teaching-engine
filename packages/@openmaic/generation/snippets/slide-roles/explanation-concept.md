**Purpose of this slide.** Teach ONE concept so the learner genuinely understands it.

**It must contain:**
- the concept, named clearly
- a rich but simplified explanation of that one concept — full sentences where the idea needs them
- the supporting ideas that make the concept make sense
- a key takeaway
- a useful visual or a small supporting example when it adds understanding

**What leads, what supports.** The concept and its core explanation lead; supporting ideas and the takeaway are visibly subordinate.

**Learner engagement.** Depth, not breadth: make the learner think about this idea from more than one angle.

**Must NOT appear:**
- a second, unrelated concept
- a list of keywords with no explanation
- a dictionary line standing in for understanding

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
