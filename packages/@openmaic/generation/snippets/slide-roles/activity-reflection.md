**Purpose of this slide.** Invite the learner to reflect.

**It must contain:**
- one clear reflection focus
- one to three meaningful, open prompts that invite personal, interpretive or connection-based thinking

**What leads, what supports.** The focus leads; the prompts are the body, with generous space.

**Learner engagement.** There is no single correct answer; the prompts open thinking.

**Must NOT appear:**
- recall or quiz-style questions
- more than a few prompts
- model answers

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
