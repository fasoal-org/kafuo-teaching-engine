**Purpose of this slide.** Let the learner apply what was already taught.

**It must contain:**
- the practice task, stated completely and unambiguously
- everything the learner needs to start (given data, the question, the expected form of the response)
- visible scaffolding only where the task genuinely needs it to be attempted

**What leads, what supports.** The task leads; any scaffolding is clearly attached to it.

**Learner engagement.** The learner does the work; the slide never does it for them.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the full solution or the final answer
- new content that was not taught earlier in the lesson

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
