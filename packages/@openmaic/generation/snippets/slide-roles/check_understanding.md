**Purpose of this slide.** A light formative check of the concept just taught.

**It must contain:**
- one focused question, or a small set of questions about the SAME recently taught concept or purpose
- everything needed to answer it

**What leads, what supports.** The question leads. Keep it focused and uncluttered.

**Learner engagement.** The learner thinks, answers for themselves or discusses. There is no answer capture on a slide.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the answer or an explanation of the answer on the canvas
- scoring, points, attempts, grades, or any assessment language
- input fields, answer boxes, option buttons or any answer-capture look-alike
- questions about different concepts

**If it does not fit.** A check is small by nature: if it needs many questions it is not a slide-based check.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
