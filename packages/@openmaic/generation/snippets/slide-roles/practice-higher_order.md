**Purpose of this slide.** Require deeper thinking about what was taught — analysis, transfer, evaluation, comparison, justification, error analysis, or application in a new context.

**It must contain:**
- a task that genuinely requires that deeper thinking, not a harder recall question
- an explicit requirement to explain or justify the reasoning
- the context or material the reasoning must work on

**What leads, what supports.** The task and the 'explain your reasoning' demand lead together.

**Learner engagement.** The learner must reason and justify. Any support available on demand helps them reason; it never replaces the reasoning.

**Must NOT appear:**
- the conclusion, the judgement, or the justification itself
- a recall question presented as higher-order
- solving guidance on the canvas

**If it does not fit.** If this cannot fit readably on one slide, present the part this slide owns completely and coherently — never overcrowd, shrink text, or abbreviate the substance; the lesson continues it on a following slide with the same purpose.

Arrangement is yours to design: choose the hierarchy, grouping and visual structure that serve this purpose best — no fixed arrangement is expected.
