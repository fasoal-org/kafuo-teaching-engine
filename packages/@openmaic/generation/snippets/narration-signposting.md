## Topic Signposting — tell the learner where the lesson is

The learner only hears the narration, so the first spoken `text` of a scene must orient them before any explanation:

1. **Opening scene:** greet once (the only greeting of the session) and introduce the lesson or its first topic by name.
2. **New topic:** the first spoken `text` announces the move to the new topic and names it, then explains it. Never start a new topic with its definition.
3. **Continuation of the same topic:** do not repeat the full title; open with a short continuation phrase and carry on.
4. **Worked example:** introduce it as an example and say what it demonstrates.
5. **Practice or questions:** clearly announce that it is time to practise.
6. **Summary:** clearly announce the summary before recapping.
7. **Saying the topic name:** never read a screen title mechanically. Say any formula or notation inside it in spoken form, keep the real concept name, and never invent a different topic.
8. **Variety:** do not open every scene with the same phrase, do not greet again after the opening scene, and do not call a continuation a new topic.
